Our Project Story

From classroom concepts to a revolutionary memory management solution

The Inspiration

During our Operating Systems course at Rajasthan Technical University, we were fascinated by the challenges of memory management and page replacement algorithms. While studying traditional approaches like LRU, FIFO, and Optimal page replacement, we realized there was an opportunity to enhance these algorithms using modern artificial intelligence techniques.

The idea for Hybrid AI-Assisted Page Replacement was born from this intersection of classical computer science fundamentals and cutting-edge machine learning technology. We wanted to create a system that could learn from memory access patterns and make smarter decisions about which pages to keep in memory.

The Challenge

Traditional page replacement algorithms operate with limited information about future memory accesses. They make decisions based on past behavior but cannot anticipate future needs effectively.

  • LRU assumes recent pages will be accessed again soon
  • FIFO doesn't consider access patterns at all
  • Optimal algorithm requires knowledge of the future
  • None adapt to changing workload patterns

Our Solution

We developed a hybrid approach that combines the reliability of traditional algorithms with the predictive power of AI:

  • GRU/LSTM neural networks predict future page accesses
  • Dynamic trust scoring evaluates prediction reliability
  • Hybrid controller combines AI suggestions with LRU
  • Continuous learning adapts to workload changes
